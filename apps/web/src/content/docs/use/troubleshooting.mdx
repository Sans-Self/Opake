<Lead>
  Opake is a decentralized protocol, which means sometimes things can get a little messy. Here is
  how to fix the most common issues you might run into.
</Lead>

## Login & Authentication

### "Invalid Handle" or "PDS not found"

If Opake can't find your account, it's usually because your handle (e.g., `you.bsky.social`) can't be resolved.

- **Check the spelling:** Ensure there are no typos in your handle.
- **Check your PDS status:** Sometimes the PDS provider you use might be down or having trouble responding to resolution requests.
- **Legacy Auth:** If your PDS doesn't support OAuth yet, you might need to use the `--legacy` flag in the CLI.

### OAuth Redirect Issues

If the browser window opens for login but never redirects you back to Opake:

- **Check for ad-blockers:** Some aggressive browser extensions might block the redirect URL.
- **CLI specific:** The CLI uses a temporary server on `127.0.0.1`. Ensure your firewall is not blocking local loopback connections.

## Files & Uploads

### Upload Fails halfway (Storage Limits)

Large files can be tricky. Opake uploads your files as single blobs to the AT Protocol.

- **Politeness Limits:** By default, Opake limits the size of blobs it will upload to respect PDS
  providers we don't own.
- **Increasing Storage:** You (or your PDS administrator) can increase these limits by uploading
  specific configuration records to your repository. Check our [Technical Spec](/docs/protocol)
  for the exact schema.
- **Network Stability:** If your connection drops, the upload may fail. Try again when you have a
  more stable connection.

### "Unable to Decrypt File"

This is the most serious error. It means Opake cannot open the "lockbox" for that file.

- **Wrong Account:** Ensure you are logged into the correct account (the one the file was shared with).
- **Missing Keys:** If you recently logged in on a new device but didn't perform a [Device Pairing](/docs/pairing), you won't have the private keys necessary to decrypt existing files.

## Sharing & Discovery

### "Recipient not found"

If you can't share a file with someone:

- **Handle vs. DID:** Ensure the handle is correct.
- **Public Key Missing:** The recipient must have logged into Opake at least once to publish their [Public Encryption Key](/docs/encryption). If they haven't done this, you cannot "wrap" a file to them.

### "I don't see shared files in my inbox"

Opake uses an **indexer** to index sharing grants.

- **Index Lag:** Sometimes it takes a few moments for the firehose to catch up.
- **Indexer Status:** Check if the indexer service is healthy. If the indexer is down, your inbox will appear empty even if the files exist.
