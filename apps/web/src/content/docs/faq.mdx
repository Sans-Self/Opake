<FaqSection>
  <FaqItem question="Is Opake free?">
    Opake itself is open-source and free to use. However, you need a **Personal Data Server (PDS)**
    to store your files. While a PDS doesn't inherently limit your storage, Opake defaults to
    smaller limits out of **politeness** to providers we don't own. You (or your provider) can
    increase these limits by uploading specific configuration files to your PDS.
  </FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Where is my data actually stored?">
  Opake uses a **bring-your-own-storage** design. Your encrypted files live on your PDS. In the
  future, we may offer managed storage options, but the goal is always to keep you in control of
  where your bits live.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can Opake see my files?">
  **No.** Encryption happens entirely on your device (browser or CLI) before any data is sent to
  internet. We never see the contents of your files, your filenames, your tags, or your private
  keys. To us, your data is just opaque noise.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Can I share files with people who don't use Opake?">
  Because of how our [Encryption & Keys](/docs/encryption) work, the recipient *must* have an
  AT Protocol identity (a DID) to receive a secure sharing [Grant](/docs/sharing). This is a
  feature, not a bug. It ensures that the file key is wrapped specifically to their public key. If
  you need to share with a total outsider, you'll need to help them join the network first—it's
  worth it for the privacy.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What happens if I lose my device?">
  If you lose your device and haven't backed up your private keys, **your data is lost forever**.
  Because Opake is end-to-end encrypted with no central authority, there is no "Forgot Password" or
  account recovery. We recommend using our [Device Pairing](/docs/pairing) feature to keep your
  identity on multiple trusted devices.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is this a blockchain?">
  **No.** Opake is built on the [AT Protocol](/docs/at-protocol), which is a federated social
  networking protocol. It uses cryptographic signatures and DIDs for identity, but it does not use a
  proof-of-work blockchain or tokens.
</FaqItem>

  <FaqItem question="How do I contribute?">
    You can [View the Sourcecode on Tangled](https://tangled.org/sans-self.org/opake.app) or [report issues](https://tangled.org/sans-self.org/opake.app/issues) there. We are built for the community, by the community.
  </FaqItem>
</FaqSection>
