## Why Opake was built on the Atmosphere

The social web most of us grew up with is broken. For years we lived in digital fiefdoms where a
single company could decide the fate of our data, our identities, and our connections. When Elon
Musk bought Twitter and spent two years dismantling its safety and moderation systems, the urgency
of a real alternative became undeniable.

The AT Protocol — and the wider Atmosphere around it — is one answer. It's an open, federated
protocol: no single person or corporation gets to own your digital life. Opake didn't invent any
of that. We picked it as our foundation because it already solves the hard parts of identity,
portability, and federation, and because building on top of an ecosystem is a better long-term bet
than trying to run our own silo.

### Breaking the silo

In the traditional cloud (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud), your identity and your storage are
permanently fused together. If you want to leave, you have to pack your bags, download
everything, and re-upload it somewhere else.

Opake uses the AT Protocol to break that cycle.

### 1. Your Digital Home (The PDS)

When you use Opake, your files are stored on your **Personal Data Server (PDS)**. Think of
this as your private home on the internet.

- You can host it yourself on your own hardware.
- You can use a trusted provider (like Bluesky).
- You can move your entire home to a new host without losing a single file.

### 2. Take Your Data With You

Because Opake speaks a universal language, your data is **portable by design**. You aren't
locked into our interface. If someone builds a better file explorer tomorrow, you can simply
log in with your handle and your files will be right there. Our code and algorithms are
public; the "vault" belongs to you, we just provide the keys.

<Callout type="info">
  **Our Guarantee:** We don't want to trap you. We want to be the best way for you to manage your
  private cabinet, but we believe you should always have the right to leave and take your data with
  you.
</Callout>

## Effortless sharing across the network

The real advantage of an open network is how easily you can connect. In traditional encrypted
apps, you have to exchange complex "invite codes" or "public keys" just to see a single file.

Since everyone in the Atmosphere already has a digital identity, sharing is built-in.

### No more invite codes

If your friends or colleagues are on Bluesky (or any other AT Protocol app), they already have a
handle. You don't need to ask for their "Opake address." You just type their handle
(`@name.bsky.social`), and Opake handles the rest.

1. Opake finds their unique digital ID.
2. It identifies their public lock on the network.
3. It creates a secure "Grant" that only their key can open.

### Collaboration Without Borders

It doesn't matter if you are with one provider and your friend is with another. The servers
talk to each other seamlessly. You can share a 1GB encrypted video with a friend on a
different host, and they can stream it directly from your cabinet—safely, privately,
and without any "middleman" watching from the sidelines.
