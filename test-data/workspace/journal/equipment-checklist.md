# Equipment Checklist

## Standard Kit
- [ ] Binoculars (10x42 minimum)
- [ ] Long-lens camera (300mm+)
- [ ] Audio recorder with directional mic
- [ ] Infrasound detector (sub-20 Hz range)
- [ ] Soil sample bags (minimum 12)
- [ ] Air quality meter (SO₂, H₂S, PM2.5)
- [ ] GPS logger with waypoint marking
- [ ] Red-light headlamp (preserves night vision)

## Protective Gear
- [ ] Full-face respirator with P100 filters
- [ ] Nitrile gloves (box of 50)
- [ ] Safety goggles (splash-rated)
- [ ] First aid kit with antihistamines

## Comfort & Logistics
- [ ] 72-hour food supply (non-perishable)
- [ ] Water filtration system
- [ ] Whiskey, cheap (1 bottle — field lure)
- [ ] Sleeping bag rated to -10°C
- [ ] Camp stove and fuel
