# Expedition Log — Spring 2026

## March 12 — Point Pleasant

Arrived at dusk. Bridge reconstruction still ongoing — good cover
for observation. Set up blind at the old munitions dump overlook.

No visual contact. Infrasound detector registered two anomalous
pulses at 22:47 and 01:13. Consistent with historical Mothman
frequency signature (18–22 Hz).

## March 15 — Braxton County

Followed up on a local report near Flatwoods. Found the scorched
circle — approximately 4m diameter, grass dead in a perfect ring.
Soil samples bagged and tagged (FL-2026-03-15-A through D).

Air quality readings showed elevated sulfur dioxide. Matches the
1952 report almost exactly. Either something was here recently,
or the original contamination is far more persistent than assumed.

## March 18 — Douglas, WY

Cross-country drive. Wyoming is beautiful and empty. Set up camp
near the original Douglas sighting coordinates.

Left the whiskey dish out at 19:00. By 03:00, it was empty.
No tracks — ground was soft enough that we should have seen them.
Audio recorder caught 47 minutes of ambient noise with three
segments of what sound like distorted human speech. Sending to
the linguistics team for analysis.
