# Flatwoods Monster Field Guide

**Classification:** Unclassified — possibly non-biological
**First sighting:** Flatwoods, West Virginia (1952)
**Threat level:** High — causes nausea and disorientation

## Identification

3+ meters tall. Dark body with a spade-shaped head or hood.
Bright red face, luminous green body. Hovers rather than walks.
Strong mechanical or chemical odor reported at all sighting locations.

## Behavioral Notes

- Always accompanied by a pulsing red light
- Produces a hissing, metallic shriek
- Proximity causes immediate nausea, throat irritation
- Leaves scorched or oily residue at landing sites

## Field Protocol

Full respirator required within 100m of sighting location.
Do not engage. Collect soil and air samples only.
Evacuate if symptoms onset — debrief at safe distance.
