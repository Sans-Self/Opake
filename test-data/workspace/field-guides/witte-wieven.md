# Witte Wieven Field Guide

**Classification:** Spiritus albae (White Women)
**First sighting:** Overijssel / Drenthe, pre-medieval oral tradition
**Threat level:** Variable — benevolent healers or malicious tricksters

## Identification

Luminous, translucent female figures in white. Typically three.
Found near burial mounds (hunebedden), fog banks, and crossroads.
Visibility peaks during dusk and pre-dawn hours, especially
around equinoxes.

## Behavioral Notes

- Guard ancient burial sites — approach with respect
- Known to heal the sick or grant prophecy to those deemed worthy
- Will mislead or trap disrespectful visitors in fog loops
- Singing or humming reported near sighting locations
- Drawn to offerings of milk, bread, or wildflowers

## Field Protocol

Never approach a hunebed after dark without an offering.
If fog thickens suddenly and unnaturally: stop walking immediately.
Turn your coat inside out (traditional countermeasure, unverified).
Document audio — their songs are the highest-priority capture.
