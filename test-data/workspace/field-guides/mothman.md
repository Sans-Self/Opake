# Mothman Field Guide

**Classification:** Lepidoptera cryptica
**First sighting:** Point Pleasant, West Virginia (1966)
**Threat level:** Ambiguous — possibly prophetic

## Identification

Approximately 2 meters tall. Distinctive red luminescent eyes,
wingspan estimated at 3–4 meters. No visible head — eyes appear
to be set directly into the upper torso.

## Behavioral Notes

- Drawn to infrastructure (bridges, power plants)
- Sightings correlate with impending structural failures
- Does not attack unless cornered
- Emits a low-frequency hum audible within 50m

## Field Protocol

Do not approach. Document from distance with long-lens.
If eyes lock onto yours: remain still, break eye contact slowly.
Report sighting to regional coordinator within 24 hours.
