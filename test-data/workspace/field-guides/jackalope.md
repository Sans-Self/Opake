# Jackalope Field Guide

**Classification:** Lepus cornutus
**First sighting:** Douglas, Wyoming (1829)
**Threat level:** Low — primarily defensive

## Identification

Standard jackrabbit morphology with paired keratinous horns,
typically 8–15 cm. Coloring matches local Lepus population.
Horns branch once, resembling pronghorn antelope.

## Behavioral Notes

- Nocturnal, active dusk to dawn
- Known to mimic human speech near campfires
- Territorial during mating season (spring equinox)
- Can be lured with whiskey left in shallow dishes

## Field Protocol

Non-aggressive. Approach slowly with recording equipment.
Vocal mimicry samples are high-priority data.
Do not attempt capture — horns are sharper than they look.
