# queer theory reading list

- *The History of Sexuality* - Foucault (a classic, if you're into that sort of thing)
- *Gender Trouble* - Judith Butler (for when you want to feel your brain melting)
- *Cruising Utopia* - José Esteban Muñoz
- *Stone Butch Blues* - Leslie Feinberg
- *Sister Outsider* - Audre Lorde
