# the roly-poly (thijs's field notes)

- actual name: armadillidium vulgare (very hard to say).
- rolls into a perfect circle when he's scared.
- 14 tiny legs, i counted them.
- he lives under the rock by the shed.
- he likes the rotten strawberries in the compost.
- anika says he's not a bug, he's a "crustacean."
